# Turnaround review: worked checks

Use this key after completing the [brief](turnaround-review.md). The example identifies one defensible trial; a different choice is defensible if it respects the same evidence and safeguards.

- **Outcome:** 20/24 = 83.3%; 14/24 = 58.3%. The deterioration is six handoffs or 25 percentage points, not 25% of 20. The prep lead needs the unit work log; a counter lead or program owner needs the actual fill and unserved cohort.
- **Ownership:** The prep crew records completion, a qualified inspector passes or holds the kit, an authorized release owner approves the match, and the counter volunteer documents pickup. An absent owner needs a named qualified delegate or a hold and timely notification. Never substitute a signature for a safety check.
- **Incentives:** A preparation target can produce a larger waiting inventory; compare queue lengths and later handoffs at fixed closing times. A safe-fill target can motivate removing difficult requests from the denominator; compare the intact intake list to the released and filled lists.
- **Capacity:** The baseline ceiling is 20, changed ceiling 14, extra preparation still 14, added signer 18, and failed batch 12. Four eligible previously inspected kits would raise passing supply to 16 and the ceiling to 16 given the same 18 approval slots, 22 counter slots and 24 eligible requests. Check eligibility and inspection status first. A mismatch between individual kit and request types or late availability may keep actual handoffs below the bound.
- **Trial:** Schedule a qualified backup for a specified window. Hold inspection, eligibility and kit mix fixed where possible. Record whether the backup really worked the slot, all confirmed requests, handoffs and exceptions. Pause on unauthorized or unsafe release. Decide whether to continue with a predeclared operating threshold, such as 17 safe fills out of 24 with no added unscheduled hours. If inspections failed, review that separate cause and run a comparable window before ascribing an effect to the backup. Do not call the result causal or evidence of satisfaction without a design that supports those claims.

**Transfer check:** If the counter loses capacity to nine after you add the signer, nine is still the ceiling. The extra release slots cannot manufacture more staffed pickup slots. Investigate the constraint you actually observe, not the one you expected.
